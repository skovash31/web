Controls
WASDMove
MouseLook around
ShiftSprint (uses stamina)
Ctrl / CCrouch (hold) — fit through vents
SpaceJump
EInteract / hide / leave hiding spot
Right-click / FFlashlight on/off (batteries you grab load automatically)
Flashlight in his eyesBlinds Mr. Thatch for 5 s (recharges in 15 s)
Left clickUse what's in your hand: fire the stapler, switch the flashlight, drink coffee
Right clickUse your off-hand item · drink coffee / use a first-aid kit you're holding
XPut the held item in your off hand (swap)
RReload what you're holding: stapler from staples (5 s), flashlight from a battery
Q / GDrop the held item (not the flashlight)
1-8 / wheelSelect inventory slot
QUse battery / first-aid kit
GDrop item (hand keycards to teammates)
JJournal (documents your team found)
T / EnterTeam chat
EscPause

Tip: Ctrl+W closes browser tabs. The game enters fullscreen with keyboard lock to protect you — or just use C to crouch.
